abstract: We study the problem of learning Bayesian network structures from data.
  We develop an algorithm for finding the k-best Bayesian network structures.
  We propose to compute the posterior probabilities of hypotheses of interest by Bayesian
  model averaging over the k-best Bayesian networks. We present empirical results
  on structural discovery over several real and synthetic data sets and show that
  the method outperforms the model selection method and the state-of-the-art MCMC
  methods.
