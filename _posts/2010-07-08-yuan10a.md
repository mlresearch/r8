abstract: A branch-and-bound approach to solving influ- ence diagrams has been previously
  proposed in the literature, but appears to have never been implemented and evaluated
  – apparently due to the difficulties of computing effective bounds for the branch-and-bound
  search. In this paper, we describe how to efficiently compute effective bounds,
  and we develop a practical implementa- tion of depth-first branch-and-bound search
  for influence diagram evaluation that outperforms existing methods for solving influence
  diagrams with multiple stages.
