abstract: 'The paper provides a simple test for deciding, from a given causal diagram,
  whether two sets of variables have the same bias-reducing potential under adjustment.
  The test re- quires that one of the following two condi- tions holds: either (1)
  both sets are admis- sible (i.e., satisfy the back-door criterion) or (2) the Markov
  boundaries surrounding the manipulated variable(s) are identical in both sets. Applications
  to covariate selection and model testing are discussed.'
