abstract: We describe a framework and an algo- rithm for solving hybrid influence
  diagrams with discrete, continuous, and deterministic chance variables, and discrete
  and continu- ous decision variables. A continuous chance variable in an influence
  diagram is said to be deterministic if its conditional distributions have zero variances.
  The solution algorithm is an extension of Shenoy’s fusion algorithm for discrete
  influence diagrams. We describe an extended Shenoy-Shafer architecture for propagation
  of discrete, continuous, and util- ity potentials in hybrid influence diagrams that
  include deterministic chance variables. The algorithm and framework are illustrated
  by solving two small examples.
