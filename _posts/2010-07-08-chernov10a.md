abstract: 'In the framework of prediction with expert advice, we consider a recently
  introduced kind of regret bounds: the bounds that de- pend on the effective instead
  of nominal num- ber of experts. In contrast to the Normal- Hedge bound, which mainly
  depends on the effective number of experts but also weakly depends on the nominal
  one, we obtain a bound that does not contain the nominal number of experts at all.
  We use the de- fensive forecasting method and introduce an application of defensive
  forecasting to multi- valued supermartingales.'
