abstract: The paper introduces k-bounded MAP infer- ence, a parameterization of MAP
  inference in Markov logic networks. k-Bounded MAP states are MAP states with at
  most k ac- tive ground atoms of hidden (non-evidence) predicates. We present a novel
  delayed col- umn generation algorithm and provide em- pirical evidence that the
  algorithm efficiently computes k-bounded MAP states for mean- ingful real-world
  graph matching problems. The underlying idea is that, instead of solv- ing one large
  optimization problem, it is often more efficient to tackle several small ones.
