abstract: We introduce a new class of lower bounds on the log partition function of
  a Markov random field which makes use of a re- versed Jensen’s inequality. In particular,
  our method approximates the intractable distri- bution using a linear combination
  of span- ning trees with negative weights. This tech- nique is a lower-bound counterpart
  to the tree-reweighted belief propagation algorithm, which uses a convex combination
  of span- ning trees with positive weights to provide corresponding upper bounds.
  We develop al- gorithms to optimize and tighten the lower bounds over the non-convex
  set of valid parameter values. Our algorithm general- izes mean field approaches
  (including na\"{}{ı}ve and structured mean field approximations), which it includes
  as a limiting case.
