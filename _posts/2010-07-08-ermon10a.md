abstract: In this paper we introduce a class of Markov de- cision processes that arise
  as a natural model for many renewable resource allocation problems. Upon extending
  results from the inventory con- trol literature, we prove that they admit a closed
  form solution and we show how to exploit this structure to speed up its computation.
  We consider the application of the proposed framework to several problems arising
  in very different domains, and as part of the ongoing ef- fort in the emerging field
  of Computational Sus- tainability we discuss in detail its application to the Northern
  Pacific Halibut marine fishery. Our approach is applied to a model based on real
  world data, obtaining a policy with a guaranteed lower bound on the utility function
  that is struc- turally very different from the one currently em- ployed.
